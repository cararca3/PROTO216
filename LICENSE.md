### Why CC BY-SA?
By choosing this license, we ensure that:
*   **Attribution:** You are free to use, modify, and build upon these designs, provided that proper credit is given to the original work.
*   **ShareAlike:** Any modifications or derivative works you create must also be shared under the same open terms. This helps keep the ecosystem free and prevents fragmentation of the standard.

We encourage you to experiment, iterate, and share your own modules. Whether you are improving an existing board or creating a new form-factor, your contributions help grow the PROTO216 community.
